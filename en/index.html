About Us

Want to boost your sales in Russia - but not sure where to start?

Sound familiar? You manufacture a great product in China - reliable electronics, stylish clothing, useful gadgets, or something else - or provide important, in-demand services. But when it comes to entering the Russian market, things get complicated: unclear legislation and taxation, a different sales market, complex target audience behaviour, a new and unfamiliar culture, and many other unanswered questions.

SEQUENCE TRADE MARKETING CONSULTING, together with our Russian partner ITS Group, helps Chinese companies confidently enter the Russian market - and achieve real sales. We do not just "provide consultations": we take care of all adaptation, marketing and promotion issues, accounting for local market specifics, cultural nuances and the competitive landscape.

Here is what you get when working with us:

  • An honest picture of the market. Before taking any action, we will study demand, analyse competitors and identify your ideal audience in Russia. No guesswork - just facts and figures.
  • A working strategy. We do not copy others solutions - we create a custom adaptation and promotion plan tailored to your product, service and budget.
  • Localisation without mishaps. We will translate and adapt everything - from labels and instructions to advertising slogans - so your brand sounds natural to Russian consumers and does not cause awkward smiles.
  • Promotion where the buyers are. We will set up ads in Yandex Direct and VK, select media outlets and bloggers with genuine audiences. We will prepare a detailed roadmap for working with Russian retail, negotiate with retail chains, launch merchandising, audits and promotional campaigns to boost sales.
  • Transparent results. We provide full support - from research and planning to campaign launch and performance monitoring. You will receive regular reports on KPIs, sales dynamics and investment efficiency.

Do not waste time and money on trial launches that might fail due to lack of market knowledge. Trust a team that has years of experience working with well-known Russian and international brands.

Want to learn exactly how we can help your business?

Contact us - and we will conduct a free 60-minute analysis of your product and the market. We will tell you which channels will deliver maximum sales and outline the first steps.

How to Enter the Russian Market: A Complete Guide

This article provides a comprehensive overview of the key steps for Chinese companies entering the Russian market, from initial market research and industry analysis to choosing the right legal structure and company registration. It helps build a clear and actionable market entry framework. We also address common compliance issues, tax requirements, and cultural differences between China and Russia, offering practical recommendations and risk mitigation strategies to reduce costly mistakes at the early stages.

From a channel and marketing perspective, we explore the local ecosystem in detail, including major e-commerce platforms such as Wildberries and Ozon, as well as digital platforms like VK and Yandex. In addition, we analyze offline retail networks and distribution systems, highlighting how different channels contribute to brand awareness, customer acquisition, and long-term growth.

The article also draws on real project experience to outline common pitfalls Chinese brands face in the Russian market, such as over-reliance on a single channel, lack of localization, and underestimating regulatory complexity. At the same time, it highlights key success factors, including refined brand positioning, strong local team collaboration, and continuous campaign optimization.

Russian Consumer Behavior Trends Analysis

This article analyzes how Russian consumers choose new brands, compare products, and make purchasing decisions. It focuses on price sensitivity, trust signals, brand reputation, and the role of reviews throughout the buying journey.

We explain how local consumers move between online and offline touchpoints, including social media, marketplaces, search engines, and retail stores. For Chinese companies, understanding these touchpoints is essential for building sharper messaging and stronger sales mechanics.

The material also shows how consumer insights can be turned into practical marketing actions: adjusting product positioning, improving content, choosing the right media channels, and building customer communication that feels natural in the local market.

VK and Yandex: Practical Digital Marketing Strategies in Russia

This article introduces two core platforms for digital marketing in Russia: VK and Yandex. It explains how companies can use search advertising, social content, retargeting, and audience segmentation to create a stable acquisition funnel.

The article clarifies which tasks each platform solves best. Yandex is especially effective for capturing existing demand and high-intent search traffic, while VK helps build brand awareness, community engagement, and creative content testing.

Through case-based examples, we show how to plan budgets, test ad creatives, measure conversion rates, and gradually optimize campaign performance. For brands entering Russia, this creates a practical and scalable digital marketing playbook.
